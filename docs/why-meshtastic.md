# Why Meshtastic in Armenia

Armenia's mountainous terrain, regional geopolitical context, and seismic history make **decentralized, off-grid communication infrastructure** essential for civic resilience.

---

## 1. 🆘 Emergency Preparedness & Disaster Resilience

Conventional telecommunications networks (GSM/LTE, fiber optic lines) have known single points of failure:
- **Grid Blackouts**: Cell towers typically rely on backup batteries that run out within a few hours during widespread power outages.
- **Earthquakes & Natural Disasters**: Cellular networks instantly saturate during earthquakes, leading to complete call and data blockage.
- **Infrastructure Vulnerabilities**: Centralized Internet backbones can be damaged, jammed, or disconnected during regional crises.

> **Meshtastic has no single point of failure.**
> Every participating node acts as a repeater. If a power outage knocks out electricity across Yerevan or a regional marz, the mesh network continues operating autonomously on battery and solar power.

---

## 2. 🏔️ Alpine Hiking, Search & Rescue, and Outdoor Sports

Armenia's mountains (Mount Aragats, Azhdahak, Khustup, Dilijan National Park, and the Lori highlands) have vast cellular blind spots:
- **Group Communication**: Keep hiking groups, backcountry skiers, and mountaineering teams connected without cellular coverage.
- **GPS Location Tracking**: Nodes with built-in GNSS automatically share coordinates over the mesh, displayed directly on offline maps in the mobile app.
- **Emergency SOS Beacons**: In case of injury, hypothermia, or lost hikers, distress beacons can hop through mountain-top repeaters into civilian or emergency channels.

---

## 3. 💸 Zero Recurring Cost & Unlicensed Frequencies

- **No SIM Cards or Monthly Fees**: Once you acquire a node ($20 - $50), operating it is completely free forever.
- **License-Free ISM Band**: Operates on the European standard **868 MHz ISM band** (`EU_868`), permitted for public use without amateur radio licensing under standard power limits.
- **Micro-Power Consumption**: Nodes draw as little as 10–20 mA, making them easily powerable by compact 5V solar panels for years of uninterrupted outdoor operation.

---

## 4. 🔒 Privacy & Military-Grade Encryption

- **Public Community Mesh**: Connect with anyone on the default public channel (`LongFast`) for neighborhood alerts and general chat.
- **Private Encrypted Groups**: Create dedicated **AES-256** encrypted channels for your family, rescue team, or organization.
- **Zero-Knowledge Relaying**: Intermediate nodes relay encrypted packets without ever being able to read their contents.
