# Welcome to Meshtastic Armenia (msh.am) 🇦🇲📡

**Meshtastic Armenia** is an independent, community-driven project building an off-grid, decentralized LoRa radio mesh across the Republic of Armenia.

Our mission is to establish a resilient, open-source communication backbone that operates 100% independently of commercial cellular carriers, fiber backbones, and centralized servers.

## ⚡ What is Meshtastic?

[Meshtastic](https://meshtastic.org) is an open-source, decentralized mesh networking system designed to run on low-power, affordable LoRa (Long Range) radios (such as ESP32 and Nordic nRF52 chips).

- 🔋 **Extreme Battery Life** — Modern nRF52 nodes can run for weeks or months on battery or small solar cells.
- 🏔️ **Long-Distance Line of Sight** — LoRa signals penetrate terrain and bridge dozens of kilometers between mountain summits and city valleys.
- 🔄 **Multi-Hop Relaying** — Every node automatically routes messages for neighbors; no central tower required.
- 🔒 **End-to-End Encryption** — Supports both public emergency channels and private AES-256 encrypted group/direct chats.
- 📱 **Zero Internet Required** — Connects via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi to your iOS/Android phone to send messages, locations, and telemetry.
